AI Daily is a personally curated digest of what happened in AI — research papers, model
releases, community discussion, and news — published daily, with a synthesized recap each week.

## Methodology

Every day, an automated sweep collects candidates from:

- **Papers** — new arXiv submissions in cs.AI / cs.CL / cs.LG and Hugging Face Daily Papers
- **Releases** — announcements from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Meta AI, Mistral, xAI, Hugging Face, and others
- **Community** — Hacker News, r/MachineLearning, r/LocalLLaMA
- **Press & newsletters** — TechCrunch AI, Simon Willison, Import AI, TLDR AI, and general tech press
- **Social** — notable X posts surfaced via web search

Candidates are deduplicated (the same story across five sources becomes one entry), scored for
significance, and reviewed by hand. Only selected items get a write-up: a summary, why it
matters, and key takeaways with links to every source that covered it.

Nothing on this site is auto-published — every entry was deliberately chosen.

## Cadence

- **Daily** — one page per day with that day's selected stories.
- **Weekly** — a synthesis of the week: cross-day themes, top papers, top news, and stats.
